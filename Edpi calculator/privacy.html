PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy is of critical importance to us. Review our data practices below to understand how we secure your configurations information.

1. Information We Collect

eDPI Calculator operates as a public utility calculation platform. We only collect details that you explicitly provide to us:

  • Contact & Feedback details: When you submit a bug report or message via our Contact Us form, we collect your name, email address, and subject matter to process your request.
  • Technical Analytics: We may collect non-identifiable browser specifications, device types, and page visit duration statistics to optimize site layouts.

2. Use of Cookies and Local Storage

We do not use tracking cookies for advertisements. However, we utilize your browser's **Local Storage** to enhance your user experience:

  • Theme Persistence: We save your light/dark mode preference in your browser's local storage (e.g. theme: 'dark') so the website renders correctly on subsequent visits.
  • Calculator Values: Your entered DPI and sensitivity values are processed locally on your machine and are not uploaded to any server.

3. Data Protection and Retention

We implement modern security practices to protect your name and email logs from unauthorized access. Contact requests are deleted periodically once resolved and are never shared with or sold to third-party marketing companies.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to other gaming pages, statistics databases, or community channels. We do not control and cannot assume responsibility for the privacy protocols and content policies of third-party platforms.

5. User Rights

Depending on your geographic location (e.g., European Union GDPR or California Consumer Privacy Act), you hold the right to request a copy of the emails you sent us or request absolute erasure of your logs. To do so, drop us a line through our standard Contact form.

6. Updates to This Policy

We reserve the right to revise this privacy document to accommodate new calculator features or regulatory amendments. The date at the bottom of our page reflects the latest revisions.
