Why eDPI Matters

In-game sensitivity is a relative multiplier. An in-game sensitivity of 0.4 means completely different movement distances if one player is running 400 mouse DPI and another is running 1600 mouse DPI. By computing your **Effective DPI (eDPI)**, you establish a universal number representing your true mechanical sensitivity:

  • Very Low eDPI (0 - 200): Best for wide arm movements, offering maximum stability and precise micro-targeting. Requires a large desk space and mousepad.
  • Low / Pro Range (200 - 400): The golden range chosen by the vast majority of Valorant professional players, balancing smooth tracking and wrist adjustments.
  • Medium eDPI (400 - 800): Ideal for wrist-oriented players, providing faster turning capabilities while maintaining solid headshot accuracy.
  • High eDPI (800+): Extremely sensitive settings, suited for players with limited desk spaces, allowing instant turns at the cost of high-precision micro-movements.
