What is eDPI?

eDPI stands for **effective Dots Per Inch**. While mouse DPI measures hardware resolution and in-game sensitivity modifies it, eDPI combines both values to show your actual physical movement output. It allows gamers with different mouse resolutions to compare their real in-game sensitivity side-by-side.

How to Calculate eDPI?

The calculation is simple: multiply your mouse hardware DPI by your in-game Valorant sensitivity settings.
eDPI = DPI × Valorant Sensitivity.
For example, if your mouse is set to 800 DPI and your in-game slider is at 0.35, your eDPI is: 800 × 0.35 = 280.

Finding Your Aim Range

Tactical shooters like Valorant require high precision. Most professional players use a Low to Medium eDPI (between 200 and 400) to make micro-adjustments easily. High sensitivity (400+) yields fast turnarounds but sacrifices consistency, while low sensitivity (below 200) requires ample arm movements.
